# V97 — Existing Subscription Plan Changes

- Existing monthly customers can now change the custom monthly amount directly in MixoLabs.
- Selecting £10 and pressing **Change Monthly Plan** updates the existing Stripe subscription instead of trying to create a duplicate.
- The selected price and new credit allowance start at the next renewal, with no surprise charge on the day of the change.
- The current monthly amount is displayed in the plan summary.
- Manage Billing remains available for cancellation and payment-method management.
