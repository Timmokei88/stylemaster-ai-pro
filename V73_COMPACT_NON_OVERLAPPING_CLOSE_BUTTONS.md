# V73 — Compact non-overlapping close controls

- Reduces the persistent X to a compact 44 px mobile touch target and 46 px desktop control.
- Reserves a dedicated control row above Terms, Privacy, FAQ, Support and Community headings on narrow screens.
- Prevents both the left-side X and right-side mobile Menu from covering page titles or content.
- Preserves the fixed position, visibility, accessibility labels and existing closing behaviour.
