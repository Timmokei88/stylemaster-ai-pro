# V101 — Family Photo Toy Transformations

- Recognises benign reference-photo requests using ordinary terms such as toy, doll, figurine, action figure, collectible, 3D animation and animated movie.
- Explicitly tells the safety classifier that transforming an ordinary photo of a child into a toy or animated character is allowed when the request contains no sexual, abusive, humiliating, violent or illegal content.
- Keeps all serious child-safety, sexual-content, exploitation, bullying, humiliation, graphic-violence and fraud protections in place.
- Distinguishes a MixoLabs moderation refusal from a downstream image-provider safety refusal.
- Automatically returns credits whenever the image provider fails to deliver an image, including provider safety refusals.
