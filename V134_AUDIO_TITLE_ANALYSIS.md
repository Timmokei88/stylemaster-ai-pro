# V134 automatic music title analysis

- Analyses every selected audio file in the owner’s browser rather than copying its filename.
- Measures audio energy, movement and tonal character and combines them with a SHA-256 audio fingerprint.
- Produces a fitting two-word title from a broad combinatorial vocabulary.
- Checks the complete loaded music library and avoids duplicate titles.
- `Suggest Another Title` produces another unique option for the same song.
- The suggested title remains editable before upload.
- Uses no customer credits and no external audio-analysis API.
- The narrated owner tutorial explains the automatic analysis and title controls.
