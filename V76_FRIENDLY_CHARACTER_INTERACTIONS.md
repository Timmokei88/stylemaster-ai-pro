# V76 — Friendly character interactions

V76 extends safe reference-photo editing to ordinary interactions such as hugging, high-fiving, shaking hands, standing beside, sitting beside, posing with, taking a selfie with, or meeting a fictional character or public figure.

Recognisable characters continue to produce the existing intellectual-property notice. The update does not override sexual-content, child-safety, nudity, exploitation, graphic-violence, harassment, humiliation, fraud, terrorism, hate or illegality classifications. Provider-level safeguards remain active.

V76 also explicitly allows background replacement and ordinary non-explicit affection between adults—including kissing, embracing and holding hands—in couple, engagement and wedding photographs. Sexualisation and non-consensual intimate manipulation remain prohibited.
