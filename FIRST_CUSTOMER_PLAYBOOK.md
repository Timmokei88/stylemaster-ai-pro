# MixoLabs: First Paying Customer Playbook

## The single goal

Do not try to “go viral” yet. The immediate goal is to find one real creator, help them make something useful with the 20-credit trial, and ask them to continue with the £1.99 pack.

## Offer

Use exactly this promise everywhere:

> Try MixoLabs with 20 free generation credits — one time only. No card required to start. Create AI art, product concepts, craft designs, tattoo ideas, realistic scenes and more. When your free credits are used, continue with a one-time pack or monthly plan.

Never say “20 free credits” without “one time only”. Do not promise that every prompt will be perfect, and do not describe the free allowance as daily or monthly.

## Best first audiences

Start with one audience at a time. Do not advertise to everyone interested in “AI”.

1. Tattoo artists and people collecting tattoo ideas.
2. Resin, felt-doll, miniature and 3D-printing creators.
3. Etsy sellers and small businesses needing product concepts or promotional images.
4. Fantasy-art, character-design and tabletop creators.
5. Interior-design and room-makeover enthusiasts.

For the first week, choose **craft and product creators**. Their use case is easy to demonstrate and they can become repeat customers.

## Seven-day routine

### Day 1 — proof

- Choose three of the strongest MixoLabs images in one niche.
- Create one 15–25 second vertical video: prompt, result, 2K enhancement, close-up.
- Put “20 FREE CREDITS — ONE TIME” on screen within the first two seconds.
- End with “No card to try • MixoLabs.art”.

### Day 2 — warm contacts

- Send a personal message to 10 people you genuinely know who create art, crafts, products or social content.
- Do not send a copied mass message. Mention why MixoLabs fits that person.
- Offer one invitation and ask them to tell you where the experience becomes confusing.

### Days 3–5 — organic publishing

- Post the video once each on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and your Facebook Page.
- Create 3–5 Pinterest Pins from the strongest result, each linking to the MixoLabs landing page.
- Join relevant Facebook or Reddit communities as a real participant. Read each community's current rules before sharing. If self-promotion is forbidden, do not post the link.
- Share a useful result and process, not “buy my product” spam.

### Day 6 — personal follow-up

- Contact everyone who accepted an invitation but did not generate an image.
- Ask one question: “What stopped you from creating your first image?”
- Help active trial users write one strong prompt in their chosen niche.

### Day 7 — conversion

- Contact users who used most or all of their trial.
- Ask what they created and offer the £1.99 one-time pack as the smallest way to continue.
- Record how many visitors registered, generated, exhausted the trial, opened pricing and paid.

## Paid advertising rule

Do not restart broad “traffic” advertising yet. Clicks without registrations only buy visits.

Paid advertising should begin only after:

- account creation is tested on mobile and desktop;
- the landing page clearly says “20 one-time credits”;
- signup, first generation and purchase events are measured;
- at least five people have completed the trial organically;
- at least one person has paid or explained precisely why they would not pay.

Then run one small test:

- Budget: £5 per day for five days (£25 maximum).
- One niche only, such as UK craft and resin creators.
- One vertical demonstration video and one before/after image.
- Optimisation goal: completed signup, not link clicks.
- Stop early if spend reaches £15 with no completed signup.
- Do not increase the budget until a campaign produces a paying customer at an acceptable cost.

## Weekly numbers

Track these in a simple table:

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Landing visitors | People who reached MixoLabs |
| Accounts created | Signup conversion |
| First generations | Activation |
| Trial credits used | Engagement |
| Pricing opened | Buying interest |
| Checkout started | Purchase intent |
| Payments completed | Customers |
| Advertising spend | Acquisition cost |

The first diagnosis is always the step with the largest drop. Fix that step before buying more traffic.
