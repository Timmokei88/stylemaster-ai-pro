# MixoLabs V108 — Benign Family and Historical Portrait Fix

- Explicitly recognises harmless requests such as “1920s kid”, Victorian child portraits and historical family-photo transformations.
- Benign generation prompts now pass MixoLabs moderation by default when no prohibited-risk wording is present.
- Uploaded family photographs still receive image safety screening.
- Harmless historical child requests are sent to the provider with clear, age-appropriate, fully clothed period-portrait wording.
- Existing protections remain for sexual content involving minors, explicit sexual content, abuse, bullying, graphic violence, terrorism, hateful degradation, fraud and other illegal exploitation.
- Provider-side safety cannot be disabled; failed provider generations continue to return credits automatically.
