# MixoLabs V148 — Advanced Generated-Image Editing

V148 expands **Edit This Generated Image** with independent controls for:

- Every generator aspect ratio: 1:1, 9:16, 16:9, 4:5, 3:4, 2:3 and 21:9.
- Every generator genre/category and its matching styles.
- Every generator theme.
- Contextual, clean white, solid black, solid white and studio-gradient backgrounds.

Changing ratio re-composes or outpaints the selected image. It explicitly prevents stretching, squashing, duplicated subjects and accidental cropping of important content. The selected generation remains the authoritative reference, the original stays available, and the edited result records its own creative settings for further edits.

An edit may be requested with written instructions, creative controls, or both. The existing one-credit confirmation remains in place.
