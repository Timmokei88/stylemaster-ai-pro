# V159 — Video Studio without Music Generator

The non-working generated-music studio has been removed from the dashboard and its generation endpoints are disabled. The existing optional uploaded background-song player remains separate.

The generator now provides a direct **AI Image / AI Video** selector. Images retain their normal displayed credit cost. Videos support every whole-second duration from 5 to 60 seconds and calculate the displayed credit cost from the owner-configured rate per started five seconds.

Video generation supports all published aspect ratios, optional exact dialogue, detailed voice-performance direction, and a consented microphone recording. Uploaded or generated reference images are passed as identity references with explicit face, cast, clothing and object continuity instructions.

Generative video and voice synthesis can vary between runs. MixoLabs strongly guides identity and delivery but does not promise mathematically exact biometric or vocal replication. Failed provider requests automatically return charged credits.
