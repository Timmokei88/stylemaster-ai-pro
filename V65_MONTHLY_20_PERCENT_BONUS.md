# MixoLabs V65 — Monthly 20% Credit Bonus

## Change

- One-time credit packs remain repeatable with no purchase limit.
- Customers can still type or select any amount from £1.99 to £1,000.
- A monthly plan now grants 20% more credits than the same-value one-time pack, rounded down to a whole credit.
- The browser preview and secure server checkout calculation use the same formula.

## Example

- £1.99 one-time: 30 credits with the Lite image-model cost setting.
- £1.99 monthly: 36 credits.

No existing credit balances or subscriptions are modified by deployment.
