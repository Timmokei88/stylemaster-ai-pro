# V75 — Safe reference-photo editing

V75 allows benign private transformations and restoration of uploaded photographs, including pictures of adults, children, relatives, friends, deceased loved ones and public figures.

Allowed examples include cheerful clown or costume edits, respectful caricatures, cartoons, 3D characters, felt dolls, superheroes, non-graphic fantasy/horror styling, restoration, enhancement, colourisation, group or commemorative composites, and clearly fictional fan-meeting scenes.

The exception is deliberately limited to reference-upload screening and cannot override sexual content involving minors, intimate abuse, bullying, humiliation, graphic violence, hateful degradation, fraud, defamation, terrorism or illegal exploitation. Publication moderation remains stricter. Provider-level generation safeguards also remain active.
