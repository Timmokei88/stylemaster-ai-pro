# V146 — Text Requests, Closable Music and Package Capacity

## Changes

- Built directly from V145 so its mobile checkout-origin correction remains intact.
- Detects prompts that explicitly request visible writing, including poems, quotations, posters, menus, invitations, labels, slogans and signs.
- Removes the contradictory `No visible writing` instruction from those requests while retaining accidental-text protection for ordinary images.
- Adds writing-specific direction for readable spelling, line breaks, contrast, word-count requests and removal of unrelated pseudo-text.
- Adds an accessible close button to the optional customer music player on desktop and mobile.
- Closing the music player immediately pauses playback and keeps the player dismissed for the current browser session.
- Keeps the deployable package below 100 files.
