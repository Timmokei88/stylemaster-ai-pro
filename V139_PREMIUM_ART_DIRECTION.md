# V139 — Accuracy-first premium art direction

## Generator quality

- Every generation now receives an accuracy-first visual-quality layer after the customer's literal request is locked.
- Photographic scenes receive professional lighting, tonal, optical, material and colour-direction guidance.
- Cartoons, caricatures, paintings, crafts and other stylised work receive medium-specific premium art direction without being forced into photorealism.
- Logos, diagrams, orthographic sheets and other graphic work receive clean geometry and legibility guidance instead of inappropriate cinematic effects.
- The quality layer explicitly prohibits sacrificing requested subjects, counts, words, viewpoints, colours, materials, positions or structural relationships for visual drama.

## Clear button

Clear now resets the browser page, the generator workspace and the internal settings column to their top positions before returning focus to the empty prompt. This makes the Prompt / Idea box immediately visible even when the customer cleared from halfway down an internal scrollbar.

No pricing, credit, safety, account, enhancement, narration or download behaviour was changed.
