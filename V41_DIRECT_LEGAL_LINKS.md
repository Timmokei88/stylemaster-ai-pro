# MixoLabs V41 — direct legal and support links

This update adds direct public URLs for Stripe receipts and other customer communications:

- `https://mixolabs.art/privacy`
- `https://mixolabs.art/terms`
- `https://mixolabs.art/support`
- `https://mixolabs.art/faq`

Each URL opens the existing corresponding MixoLabs panel automatically. Closing the panel returns the browser to the homepage. Existing footer, account and Settings buttons continue to work unchanged.

## After deployment

Open each direct URL in a private browser window and confirm the correct panel appears. Then update Stripe's public details:

- Customer support URL: `https://mixolabs.art/support`
- Privacy policy URL: `https://mixolabs.art/privacy`
- Terms of service URL: `https://mixolabs.art/terms`

Keep the business website as `https://mixolabs.art`.
