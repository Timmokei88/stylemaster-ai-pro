# V135 — cleaner generated scenes and descriptive downloads

- Images with no requested lettering now explicitly prohibit stray words, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks and pseudo-text.
- Environmental signs are requested as blank, turned away, cropped, naturally obscured or softened by depth of field.
- When Exact Text is supplied, only that wording is requested and all additional stray lettering is prohibited.
- Original, enhanced and print downloads use a safe descriptive filename derived from the prompt that produced the selected image.
- The selected generation retains its original prompt metadata even if the prompt field is edited before download.

Example: `a red-haired woman walking on a rainy London street` downloads as:

- `mixolabs-red-haired-woman-walking-rainy-london-street.png`
- `mixolabs-red-haired-woman-walking-rainy-london-street-2k-enhanced.jpg`

AI generation remains probabilistic. These controls materially reduce accidental lettering but cannot guarantee flawless text, anatomy or composition in every provider output.
