# V82 — Maker tutorials

- Adds written tutorials for resin printing, filament printing, digital sculpting and hand clay sculpting.
- Uses locally generated, process-specific guidance, so creating the written guide consumes no image credits and makes no additional AI text request.
- Reuses the existing female and male natural narration endpoint, with browser narration as a fallback.
- Clearly distinguishes an AI concept image from a printable 3D mesh.
- Marks printer settings as approximate and requires manufacturer profiles, calibration tests and professional verification for functional or safety-critical work.
