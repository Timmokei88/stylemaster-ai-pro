# MixoLabs V69 — Proportional Pack and Monthly Credits

The entire £1.99–£15 adjustable payment scale now uses the conservative 5p allowance while rounding the one-time allowance up to the next whole credit. Monthly subscriptions receive 20% more than the matching one-time amount, also rounded up.

Examples:

| Customer amount | One-time pack | Monthly plan |
|---:|---:|---:|
| £1.99 | 25 credits | 30 credits |
| £2.69 | 34 credits | 41 credits |
| £3.50 | 46 credits | 56 credits |
| £4.99 | 67 credits | 81 credits |
| £7.00 | 95 credits | 114 credits |
| £10.00 | 137 credits | 165 credits |
| £15.00 | 208 credits | 250 credits |

This is proportional: the monthly advantage grows with the selected payment rather than adding a flat five credits to every tier. The browser preview and secure server checkout use the same calculation.

V69 contains all earlier Early Access, waiting-list, £15 limit and mobile-navigation updates.
