# MixoLabs V78 — CAD and Blueprint Tools

## Added

- A dedicated **CAD & Technical Design** category with ten technical visual styles.
- A **CAD & Blueprint Tools** panel beneath the main generator settings.
- Technical modes for 3D CAD concepts, blueprints, exploded assemblies, orthographic sheets, cutaways and patent-style drawings.
- Measurement units and an optional specifications field for dimensions, parts, materials and assembly notes.
- Automatic detection when prompts mention CAD, blueprints, schematics, exploded assemblies, orthographic views or cutaways.
- Strict handling that reproduces supplied dimensions and marks unspecified values instead of inventing them.
- An optional conceptual-estimate mode that visibly labels every estimate and the whole output as not for fabrication.

## Important limitation

Outputs are visual concepts rather than editable CAD files or certified engineering drawings. Geometry, dimensions, structure, tolerances, assembly and regulatory requirements must be checked in professional CAD software by a qualified person before construction or manufacture.

## Example

Prompt: `Create an exploded 3D CAD-style breakdown of a recreational speedboat, showing the hull, deck, propulsion, steering, electrical system, fuel system and seating assembly.`

Then enter verified dimensions in **Measurements, parts and assembly notes**, select the appropriate units and choose **Use only my measurements**.
