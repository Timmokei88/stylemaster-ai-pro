# MixoLabs V106 — Free Enhancer and Lighting Consistency

- Image generation is fixed to Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite at 1K and costs one credit per image.
- The unsupported 2K and 4K provider-generation paths are disabled.
- Generated images now include a free local 2×/4× enhancement workspace.
- The enhancer has a draggable before/after comparison, enhanced PNG download, and print JPEG download with 300 DPI metadata.
- Enhancement runs in the customer’s browser and does not debit credits or call the image provider.
- The core prompt now explicitly requires consistent light sources, cast shadows, contact shadows, reflections, ambient occlusion and subject grounding.

The enhancer improves enlargement and apparent clarity. It is deliberately described as enhancement rather than native AI-generated 2K/4K detail.
