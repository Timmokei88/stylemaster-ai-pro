# MixoLabs V151 — Accurate Video Audio

- Video prompts now demand frame-accurate synchronisation between visible actions and their sounds.
- Audio direction, distance, loudness, material character and environmental reverberation must match the scene.
- Music, speech and narration are suppressed unless explicitly requested.
- Unrelated effects, generic ambience, incorrect animal sounds and audio discontinuities are prohibited.
- The customer interface now explains how to request an exact sound and gives a clear example.
- Existing automatic refunds, owner controls, prices and video formats remain unchanged.

Generative audio can still vary between runs, so MixoLabs must not promise infallible output. Failed provider requests continue to return credits automatically.
