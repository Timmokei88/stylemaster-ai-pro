# MixoLabs V57 — Readable UI and Legal Narrator

## Improvements

- The main Prompt / Idea box now starts larger and automatically expands downward as more text is entered.
- The prompt stops growing at a practical maximum height and then becomes scrollable.
- Small supporting text in Plans & Credits has been increased for readability, including payment notes, plan details, consent wording, and billing status messages.
- The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions now include a page-level narrator with female and male voice choices.
- Long legal pages are narrated in manageable sections with a visible section-progress message and a Stop control.
- Existing generation, billing, moderation, account, support-bot, and automated-email functionality is unchanged.

## Deployment

Deploy this package in the same way as the previous production package, retain the existing production environment variables, and publish the completed deployment.
